About Life Link

Love for life, hope for every day

Gugma sa Kinabuhi, Paglaum sa Matag Adlaw.

Life Link stands for the ties that keep a patient going: patients, families, health workers, local leaders and community partners linked together around the value of life. We propose Cebu as the pilot for an integrated kidney care model.

Vision

To make Cebu the starting point of a new standard in Philippine renal care, helping patients live longer and better while developing Filipino dialysis professionals for opportunities at home and around the world.

To cultivate Cebu as the Regional Healthcare Destination for 2030.

Mission

To help Filipino renal patients live longer and better through accessible, high-quality and comprehensive kidney care, while developing world-class Filipino dialysis nurses through education, USRN mentorship, local employment and global career opportunities.

5-year roadmap

International expertise · Local clinical leadership · Community partnership · Sustainable healthcare development

  1. 2027

    Foundation and Cebu pilot

    Organization, partnerships, permits, financing, site and the first integrated dialysis center.

  2. 2028

    Stabilization and validation

    Prove quality outcomes and sustainability, and build the nursing workforce.

  3. 2029

    Cebu expansion

    Bring the model to more high-need Cebu communities.

  4. 2030

    Regional expansion

    Grow beyond Cebu and strengthen nurse training and global career pathways.

  5. 2031

    Replicable Philippine model

    A scalable renal care and dialysis workforce platform.

LGU and private partnership

Clear roles, shared results

The LGU connects the program to the community and public health system. Life Link brings the clinical expertise, equipment, technology and staff. The partnership does not move the full cost or work to the LGU.

LGU · community support

Site support where applicable · transport and fuel support by agreement · local and barangay coordination · patient identification and referral · indigent patient assistance · community health promotion

Life Link · clinical and operations

Dialysis machines · water treatment system · medical equipment and supplies · nephrologist, nurses and dialysis staff · quality assurance · infection control · maintenance · clinical records

Joint · progress report

Patient monitoring · program evaluation · community education · referral coordination · quality improvement · expansion programs · financial and program reporting
